## Museums and history

The British Museum is in Bloomsbury and has collections from many places and
periods. The National Gallery is near Trafalgar Square. These sites can suit
an interest in history or art. Source: Visit London.

## Riverside and culture

The South Bank and Bankside have riverside walks and cultural sites. Tate
Modern and Shakespeare's Globe are in this area. A traveler can choose one
indoor site and leave time for a walk. This is demo planning guidance, not a
travel-time estimate. Source: Visit London.

## Before travel

London covers a large area. Check the route and access for each selected site.
Check admission rules and any booking need with the venue. This file does not
give live service, weather, price, or crowd data.
