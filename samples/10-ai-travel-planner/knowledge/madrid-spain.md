## Art and museums

Paseo del Prado has the Prado Museum and other cultural sites. It can suit a
traveler with an interest in art. Source: Tourism Madrid.

## Parks and walking

El Retiro Park is near the Paseo del Prado area. A traveler can pair one
museum with time in the park. This is demo planning guidance, not a statement
about walking time, access, or the weather. Source: Tourism Madrid.

## Before travel

Check museum admission, park access, and transport before you choose a plan.
This file does not give live opening times, prices, weather, or crowd levels.
