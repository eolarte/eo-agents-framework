## Ancient history

The Colosseum Archaeological Park includes the Colosseum, Roman Forum, and
Palatine. These sites can suit a traveler with an interest in ancient history.
Source: Turismo Roma.

## Art and outdoor time

Villa Borghese has park grounds. Galleria Borghese is in this area and has
art collections. A traveler can choose an art visit or outdoor time, based on
their interests. This choice is demo planning guidance. Source: Turismo Roma.

## Before travel

An outdoor site can need more walking than an indoor visit. Check the route,
access, entry rules, and weather before you go. This file does not give live
prices, schedules, travel times, or crowd levels.
