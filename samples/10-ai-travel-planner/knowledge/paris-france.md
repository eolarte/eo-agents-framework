## Art and history

The Louvre is a major art museum. The Eiffel Tower and Notre-Dame are major
landmarks. Montmartre has streets and viewpoints that can suit a walking day.
Use the Louvre for an indoor art option. Source: Paris tourism office.

## Outdoor time

The Seine links several central landmarks. A walk by the river or a river
boat trip can add outdoor time to a museum day. A traveler who prefers a slow
pace can select one landmark and one nearby area for a day. This last sentence
is demo planning guidance, not a route or travel-time estimate.

## Before travel
