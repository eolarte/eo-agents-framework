## Art and museums

The Museumplein area has the Rijksmuseum and other museums. It can suit a
traveler with an interest in art. Source: I amsterdam.

## Canals and streets

The canal ring is a central part of the city. The Nine Streets area has small
streets, shops, and canal views. A traveler who prefers a slow pace can choose
one museum and one area for a walk. This is demo planning guidance, not a
route or travel-time estimate. Source: I amsterdam.

## Before travel

Check access, admission, and any booking need with the site. This file does
not give live canal service, weather, prices, or crowd levels.
