## Museums and history

Museum Island is a group of historic museums in central Berlin. It can suit
an interest in art and cultural history. The Brandenburg Gate is a city
landmark. The Reichstag area is near the gate. Source: visitBerlin.

## Planning notes

For a slow day, select one museum and allow time for nearby streets. For a
history day, use the Brandenburg Gate area as one focus. These are demo
planning examples. They do not state opening times or travel times.

## Before travel

Check which museum buildings and collections are open before you choose a
visit. Check access and ticket rules with the site. This file does not give
live weather, prices, transport service, or crowd levels.
