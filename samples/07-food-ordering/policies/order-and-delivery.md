# Order and delivery demonstration policy

This fictional restaurant accepts local demonstration orders for pickup or for
delivery to Central, Riverside, and North Park. Estimated delivery times are
25-40 minutes in Central, 35-50 minutes in Riverside, and 40-55 minutes in
Riverside and North Park. The MCP restaurant information tool is the source of
the sample's delivery zone and estimate data.

An order draft becomes confirmed only after the customer explicitly confirms
it. Confirmation changes local sample state only. The sample does not send an
order to a restaurant, contact a courier, or charge a payment method.
