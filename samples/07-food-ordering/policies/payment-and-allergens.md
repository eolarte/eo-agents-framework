# Payment and allergen demonstration policy

The sample supports two fictional payment choices: `demo-card` and `cash`.
`demo-card` is only a label used to demonstrate validation. Never enter or
store a real card number, security code, bank login, or other payment secret.
No payment is processed.

Menu descriptions list selected ingredients and dietary tags, but the sample
does not maintain a verified allergen database or kitchen cross-contact
information. Customers with allergies should be told that the available demo
content cannot establish whether an item is safe for them.
