# Northstar Works payroll policy (fictional demonstration)

- Employees are paid monthly on the last business day of the month.
- The May 2026 statement covers work performed from May 1 through May 31, 2026.
- The demo employee's monthly base salary is 5,000 payroll credits (PC).
- The employee receives a fixed monthly home-office allowance of 500 PC. For this demonstration, the allowance is included in gross pay and taxable pay.
- The employee contributes 5% of base salary to the company retirement plan before income tax is calculated.
- Payroll amounts are shown in payroll credits (PC), an invented demo currency with no real-world value.
