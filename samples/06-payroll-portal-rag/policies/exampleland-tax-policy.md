# Exampleland payroll tax policy (fictional demonstration)

- Exampleland is an invented country created only for this sample.
- For this demo, income tax is a flat 10% of taxable pay after the employee retirement contribution is deducted.
- For this demo, social insurance is 4% of gross pay before deductions.
- This sample uses no tax credits, additional brackets, employer-side contributions, or other adjustments.
- These simplified rules are invented and do not describe any real country's tax system.
