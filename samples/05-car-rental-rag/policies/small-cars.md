# Small car rental policy (demonstration)

- Drivers must be at least 21 years old and have held a valid driving licence for at least one year.
- The renter must present a valid driving licence and a payment card in the main driver's name at pickup.
- Additional drivers must be registered on the rental agreement before driving.
- The car may be driven on paved public roads only. Off-road driving and towing are not permitted.
- Return the car with the same fuel level recorded at pickup. A refuelling service fee applies if it is returned with less fuel.
- Report accidents, damage, or mechanical problems to the rental desk as soon as possible.
