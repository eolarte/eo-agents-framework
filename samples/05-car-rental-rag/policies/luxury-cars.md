# Luxury car rental policy (demonstration)

- Drivers must be at least 28 years old and have held a full driving licence for at least five years.
- The renter must present a valid driving licence and a payment card in the main driver's name at pickup.
- A higher refundable security deposit is required for this category. The exact amount is confirmed at booking.
- Only registered drivers may operate the vehicle. Additional drivers must be approved and added to the agreement before pickup.
- Track-day use, racing, towing, and off-road driving are not permitted.
- The vehicle must be returned to the agreed location and reported damage must be reviewed with rental staff.
