# Electric car rental policy (demonstration)

- Drivers must be at least 23 years old and have held a valid driving licence for at least two years.
- The renter must present a valid driving licence and a payment card in the main driver's name at pickup.
- The car is supplied with at least an 80% battery charge. Return it with at least a 30% charge; a recharge service fee applies if it is returned below that level.
- Only use compatible public charging equipment. Report charging faults or damage to the rental desk.
- Do not tow with the vehicle or use it for off-road driving.
- Additional drivers must be registered on the rental agreement before driving.
