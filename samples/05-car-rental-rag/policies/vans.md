# Van rental policy (demonstration)

- Drivers must be at least 25 years old and have held a valid driving licence for at least two years.
- The renter must present a valid driving licence and a payment card in the main driver's name at pickup.
- The van may not exceed its posted passenger or cargo weight limits. Secure all cargo before driving.
- Passenger vans may carry only the number of occupants supported by installed seats and seat belts.
- Towing is not permitted unless the rental agreement explicitly lists an approved towing package.
- Additional drivers must be registered on the rental agreement before driving.
